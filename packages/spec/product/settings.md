# Settings

**Purpose.** Where a brewer reaches the app's less-common actions — the ones that don't earn a
permanent spot in the primary navigation — from one place, wherever they are in the app.

## Behaviours

**SETTINGS-01** — A **Settings** control is reachable from anywhere in the app, shown as an icon
a brewer can tap or click.

**SETTINGS-02** — Tapping or clicking the Settings control opens a menu of less-common actions.

**SETTINGS-03** — The Settings menu's first entry is **Backup**.

**SETTINGS-04** — Choosing **Backup** from the Settings menu takes the brewer to the Backup
screen.

## Known gaps

_None._

## Out of scope

- Any Settings menu entry beyond Backup — today it's the only one; more is future work.
- A dedicated Settings screen or landing page — the Settings control opens a menu, not a screen
  of its own.
